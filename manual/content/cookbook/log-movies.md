Start with **Movies** for a film or **TV & Drama** for a series. Add *LoveLive! The School Idol Movie* through [Quick Capture](../features/adding.mdx#quick-capture).

## Record a viewing

Set a watched date and rating, then use [Log activity](../features/log.mdx) for a note you want to remember:

```markdown
- [[LoveLive! The School Idol Movie]] — watched with friends #Movies
```

For a rewatch, add another log. This keeps each viewing in your history instead of using one date field to represent every viewing.

A series can also use an [episode checklist](../features/episodes.mdx). Keep seasons together or separate according to whether you want one rating or a rating per season.

Link related entities to a **LoveLive!** franchise entity. To bring in a collection from Trakt or an IMDb export, use [Batch import](../features/import.mdx).
