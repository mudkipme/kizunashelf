Use **Books** for books and **Manga & Comics** for manga. For example, add *Pokémon Adventures* to your manga collection and mark it as reading.

## Choose a useful unit

Use chapter progress if you read chapter by chapter. Keep separate entities for volumes if you want individual ratings, purchase notes, or completion dates. You do not need to track both unless both are useful to you.

Record a reading session with [Log activity](../features/log.mdx):

```markdown
- [[Pokémon Adventures]] — read another chapter #Manga
```

When you finish, add a completion date. For a reread, daily notes preserve the new sessions alongside the old ones.

Link the series to a **Pokémon** franchise entity to find it alongside your games. For a collection already on Goodreads, see [Batch import](../features/import.mdx).
