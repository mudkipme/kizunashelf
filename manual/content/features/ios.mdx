Use KizunaShelf on iPhone or iPad to browse and edit your vault without running a server. Keep the vault on your device or open a folder from Files. For offline use, make sure cloud files have downloaded first; searches, imports, and remote covers still need a connection.

## Coming Up widget

Add the Coming Up widget to your Home Screen to see [upcoming releases and episodes](./calendar.mdx#whats-coming-up).

## Notifications & reminders

Turn on reminders for releases and airing episodes you are waiting for. Your reminder preferences stay on this device.

## Spotlight search

Search for an entity from the iPhone or iPad Home Screen and open its detail page directly.

## Share-sheet capture

Share a provider page from Safari to KizunaShelf to open [Quick Capture](./adding.mdx) with the URL already filled in.

## Siri, Shortcuts & the Action button

Use KizunaShelf's actions in Shortcuts to capture an entity or [log activity](./log.mdx). You can run a shortcut with Siri or assign it to a supported iPhone's Action button.

## Background downloads

Cover downloads can continue when you leave the app. You can return later to check their progress.

To get started, follow the [iOS quickstart](../start/quickstart.md#ios). For cloud folders and offline access, see [Syncing your vault](../guides/syncing.md#ios).
