Download covers into your vault to keep them available offline. A local copy also stays available if the original image URL stops working.

## Download a cover

[Quick Capture](./adding.mdx) tries to download the cover when it creates an entity. You can also apply a cover while [matching an existing entity](./editing.mdx#matching-external-metadata).

## Download several covers

After an [import](./import.mdx), open **Download remote covers** on the Review page. Choose one type or the whole library. The downloader finds image fields with online URLs and saves local copies.

If a cover is missing entirely, [match the entity to a provider](./editing.mdx#matching-external-metadata) or add an image first.

The image fields then point to files in your vault's assets folder. Include that folder when you [sync or back up your library](../guides/syncing.md).

For images hosted on your own network, see the [self-hosted server setting](../reference/web-server.md#configuration) for private asset hosts.
