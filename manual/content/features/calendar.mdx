Use the **calendar** to see dates at a glance and the **activity feed** to look through your history or plan what comes next.

## What's coming up?

Release dates, event dates, and scheduled episodes appear on the calendar. Dates need a specific day to appear in the month grid; an airing season appears in upcoming views instead.

Home's **Coming up** panel shows the next releases and episodes. On iOS, you can also use a [widget or reminders](./ios.mdx).

## What have I done recently?

The activity feed brings together episode check-offs, start and finish dates, and mentions in your daily notes. Each activity links back to its entity.

Choose **Recent** for recent activity or **All** for the complete record.

## What should I catch up on?

- **Up next** shows future plans and scheduled episodes you have not watched. Completed, dropped, and paused entities are left out.
- **Catch up** shows released entities still on your planning list and aired episodes of shows you are watching.

A show airing this season can appear in both: some episodes are available, while others are still ahead. Marking an episode watched or changing an entity's status updates the lists.

If a date is missing from these views, check its [role in the schema](../reference/titles-dates-status.md#dates-and-calendar-design).
