## Criteria

Open **Filter** to add rules. For example:

- Anime you are watching.
- Games rated 8 or higher.
- Entities linked to the *Pokémon* franchise.
- Things you finished in the last 30 days.

Combine rules to match all, any, or none of them. Choose a type to filter by its fields; **All types** offers shared details such as titles, update times, and tags.

### Seasons

A season filter recognizes equivalent values such as `Spring 2018` and `2018年春`. You can choose one season, several seasons, or a whole year.

## Search

Search matches all saved titles, including titles in other languages, as well as summaries and file paths. It narrows the current results alongside your filters. Results rank by relevance unless you choose a sort order.

## Sorting

Sort by title or last update, or choose a type's date, rating, or progress field. Entities with no value for that field appear last.

## Keep a useful view

Choose **Save as smart list** to keep your filters and view settings. The search text is not saved with the list. See [Lists & smart lists](./lists.mdx) for editing saved lists and adding them to Home.

On web and desktop, your current browsing choices stay in the page URL, so reload and the Back button preserve them.

## Titles follow your language

KizunaShelf displays the title matching your language preference when one is available, with the original title as a fallback. All titles remain searchable. The [title reference](../reference/titles-dates-status.md#title-design) explains how to configure them.
