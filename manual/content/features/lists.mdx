Use a regular list when you want to choose and order entities yourself. Use a **smart list** when you want filters to choose them for you.

## Lists

A list can hold your favorite Pokémon games, a rewatch order, or albums you want to share with a friend. Add entities, drag them into order, and write notes alongside them.

## Smart lists

A smart list updates whenever your library changes. A *Watching Anime* list, for example, drops an entity when you mark it completed.

Create one in **Lists**, or choose **Save as smart list** while [browsing](./browse.mdx). Use **Edit criteria** to change its filters. Views let you keep different layouts and sorts in the same list.

### Seasons

Season rules work the same way as [library filters](./browse.mdx#seasons). A saved rule for Spring 2018 matches both English and Japanese ways of writing that season.

## Show a list on Home

Open a smart list and choose **Add to Home**. See [Home & smart lists](./home.mdx) for suggested lists and how Home previews your views.

## The Obsidian bridge

Lists live under `KizunaShelf/Lists/` in your vault. Regular lists are Markdown files with wikilinks. Smart lists use Obsidian's `.base` format, so you can open supported views in Obsidian too.

KizunaShelf supports a subset of Bases features and preserves settings it cannot edit. Season matching can differ: Obsidian compares the written text, while KizunaShelf recognizes the season it names. Keep season spellings consistent if you use the list in both apps.
