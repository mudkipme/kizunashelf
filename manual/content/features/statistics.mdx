## Statistics

See totals for entities and their connections, how your library breaks down by type, and where its links come from. The activity heatmap shows dates across your library, including plans and releases as well as past activity.

For the individual entries behind your history, open the [activity feed](./calendar.mdx#what-have-i-done-recently).

## Review: cleanup queues & library diagnosis

Use Review to find missing covers, broken assets, unmatched or unlinked entities, and duplicate filenames. Open a queue to work through its notes. To save covers that already have online URLs, use [Download remote covers](./covers.mdx).

Library diagnosis also reports files the app could not read and links it could not resolve. These reports help you locate the affected notes; they do not change the files for you.
